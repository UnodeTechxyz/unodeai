# Privacy and DPA Review

1. Name the framework you are reviewing against (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or an internal standard). A
   review with no named framework has no criteria and cannot be checked.
2. Identify the roles the document assigns -- controller, processor, joint controller -- and quote the text
   that assigns them. A role mislabel changes every downstream obligation.
3. Record the categories of personal data, data subjects, and processing purposes as the document states
   them. Mark anything the document leaves open-ended.
4. For cross-border transfers: identify the mechanism claimed (adequacy, SCCs, BCRs, derogation), the exact
   module or clause cited, and whether a transfer impact assessment is referenced. **Do not assess whether a
   mechanism is currently valid** -- validity changes with regulatory decisions and is a legal determination.
5. Check for, and quote or mark absent: sub-processor authorisation and notice, retention and deletion
   terms, security measures, audit rights, data-subject-request assistance, and breach-notification timing.
6. Produce a table of `present / absent / ambiguous` with a span for every `present`.
7. **Stop at the boundary.** This is a completeness review against a checklist, not a legal opinion on
   adequacy, and not a sign-off. A privacy or legal owner decides. Say so in the output.
