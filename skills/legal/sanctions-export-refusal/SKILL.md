# Sanctions and Export Control -- analyse, never clear

A wrong clearance here is a criminal-liability exposure for the organisation and for individuals. Research and
analysis help the person who decides; a determination you present as final would be relied on as a clearance.
So do the analysis, and leave the decision with a person who has the authority to make it.

1. Recognise the subject: restricted or denied party screening, embargoed or sanctioned destinations,
   controlled or dual-use technology classification, deemed export to foreign nationals, end-use or end-user
   restrictions, or a licence requirement.
2. **Do the research the person asked for.** Read the sources you can reach: the published lists and their
   official search tools, the regulations, the classification lists, and the documents already held. Quote what
   each source says and where it says it, with the date you read it.
3. **Analyse, with stated uncertainty.** Say which entries look like possible matches and why (name, aliases,
   address, identifiers), which classification entries look relevant and why, and what facts would change the
   answer. Mark what you could not check.
4. **Never clear.** Do not say that a party is cleared or not listed, that an item's classification is final,
   or that a transaction is permitted. Write "possible match", "no match found in <source> on <date>", or
   "candidate classification", and say what a qualified review still has to confirm.
5. State the specific question the decision-maker must answer, so they are not handed a pile of context and
   asked to work out what was being asked.
6. Name the decision owner: the export-control or trade-compliance owner if one is named, otherwise say that a
   qualified owner must make this decision before anything proceeds.
7. **Put the analysis, its sources, its limits and the open decision in your reply.** The reply is the record
   that always exists, and it is the one you can always write: several roles that run this skill are read-only
   by design. Ask the coordinator or the decision owner to keep it wherever the work is tracked, and say that
   you could not do it yourself if you have no write capability.
