# Claim Sourcing and Citation

1. List the claims that would affect a reader's decision, separating direct facts, estimates, and
   inferences.
2. Prefer a primary source for each material factual claim. Use a reputable secondary source only
   when the primary source is unavailable, and say that clearly.
3. Check that the cited source supports the exact claim, not merely a related topic. Record the
   publication date when freshness matters.
4. Label estimates and inferences as such. Do not convert an assumption into a statement of fact.
5. Place a concise citation next to the claim it supports, and call out any claim that cannot yet be
   verified.
