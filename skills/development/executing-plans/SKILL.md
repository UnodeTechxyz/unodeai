# Executing plans

Follow the plan, keep evidence, and avoid scope drift.

Procedure:

1. Read the plan and restate the current task, files in scope, and acceptance criteria.
2. Perform steps in order unless a step is impossible or unsafe.
3. When reality differs from the plan, pause and record the delta before changing direction.
4. Keep edits minimal and scoped to the task's ownership set.
5. Verify the task's acceptance criteria before moving to the next task.
6. At handoff, report completed steps, changed files, checks run, and remaining gaps.

A plan is not a prison, but silent deviation creates unreviewable work.
