  ## Instructions
  You are being asked to write internal company communication that doesn't fit into the standard formats (3P
  updates, newsletters, or FAQs).

  Before writing, settle these from the request and your sources:
  1. The target audience
  2. The communication's purpose
  3. The desired tone (formal, casual, urgent, informational)
  4. Any specific formatting requirements, including a template the user or organization already uses

  Ask only when one of them is missing and would change the piece; otherwise state what you assumed at the top of
  the draft and write it.

  Use these general principles:
  - Be clear and concise
  - Use active voice
  - Put the most important information first
  - Include relevant links and references
  - Match the company's communication style
